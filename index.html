What you need to know before requesting a strategy conversation.

How does education-led content support revenue?

It can help people discover products and services, understand their value, compare options, justify a decision, implement successfully and continue using what they buy. It also gives revenue-facing teams reusable material. This supports the path to revenue but does not promise an outcome.

How does the 45-day Content Sprint work?

Kryssen agrees the decision, evidence and content deliverables before work begins. The first completed asset is scheduled by Day 21, with the agreed related asset set completed through Day 45, subject to the client providing access, evidence and approvals on schedule.

What is included in the Monthly Retainer?

The retainer covers agreed monthly priorities, ongoing content strategy, recurring content creation, adaptation for selected distribution routes and a review of performance and next priorities. The monthly scope and capacity are agreed before the retainer begins.

When should we choose Project-Based?

Choose Project-Based when your company already knows the specific website, decision, sales, executive, LinkedIn or customer-learning material it needs. Kryssen agrees a fixed scope, review process and delivery schedule.

What is included in the Content Sprint?

Strategy for one buyer or customer decision, a focused set of related finished content assets, two structured review rounds and agreed handover files. The exact assets, quantity, owners and delivery dates are named in the scope before work begins.

Who decides which content assets are created?

Kryssen recommends the focused asset set after examining the buyer or customer decision, available evidence and commercial priority. The company approves the scope before creation begins.

Does Kryssen publish the content?

Publication responsibility is agreed in the scope. Kryssen can prepare production-ready files and, where the required access and platform support are available, place agreed assets into the relevant publishing workflow. The company retains final approval.

What happens if our team delays access, evidence or approvals?

The delivery dates move by the time lost unless both sides agree another recovery plan. The scope identifies the client owner, evidence, expert time and approval dates required to protect the schedule.

What happens after an engagement?

You receive the finished assets and agreed handover documentation. Your team can use the content internally, move to a Monthly Retainer, commission another project or stop. There is no automatic renewal.
