Four truths that shape the build.

These are the operating realities behind the Revenue Breakout. We design for them instead of pretending B2B revenue behaves like consumer campaign volume.

01

The buyer is not one person.

You need the user, champion, blocker and budget owner to move, not a broad audience.

02

A signup is not revenue.

PLG fails when users do not activate, stay, pay or create a sales-ready account.

03

An MQL is not pipeline.

Sales-led growth needs accounts with fit, authority and a real reason to buy now.

04

A campaign is not a motion.

Revenue needs demand, product, sales and measurement to work as one system.

The motion depends on where your break lives.

Before you apply.

What do we get in 45 days?

Senior-led diagnosis, one-motion plan, the live demand path, qualification or product-signal rules, measurement, one controlled improvement cycle and a documented Scale / Fix / Kill decision.

How does the 50 / 30 / 20 structure work?

50% starts the engagement. 30% is paid when the system is live by day 21. The final 20% stays held until the named commercial proof event is verified.

Who does the work?

Ogunlade Oluwole and Idorenyin Idiong lead the work directly. This is senior operator work, not a junior handoff.

Why only two companies per cycle?

Because the work is senior-led and implementation-heavy. We keep intake intentionally small so the operators building the system are the same operators reviewing the motion and making the calls.

What do you need from our team?

One executive decision-maker, one day-to-day revenue owner, access to relevant CRM or product data, and prompt approvals during the 45 days.

Are the logos Kryssen clients?

No. They represent cumulative operator experience across employment, founder, advisory and consulting roles. The proof pages state the role context before the metrics.

What counts as proof?

A motion-specific commercial event: accepted opportunity for sales-led, activated or paid account for PLG, or product-qualified account progressing toward revenue for hybrid.

Do you replace our team?

No. We build the motion with your team and tighten the connection between demand, product and sales so your operators can run it after the sprint.

Do you guarantee revenue?

No. We guarantee the agreed build, launch and proof process. Your product, pricing and team execution also affect the commercial result.

What happens after day 45?

You get a Scale / Fix / Kill decision and the operating documentation. Keep it in-house or scope the next phase separately.
