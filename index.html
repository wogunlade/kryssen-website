The questions that matter before either of us says yes.

What exactly are we buying?

A 45-day Education-Led Growth build: the buyer questions, education structure, first answers, relevant distribution and measurement rules needed to make the system operational. Exact scope follows the fit review.

What counts as proof?

An observable commercial event agreed before the work begins—such as an accepted opportunity, activated account or product-qualified progression—read alongside what the education is doing in search, AI and owned channels. It is not a revenue promise.

What does it cost, and how is it paid?

One fixed price agreed at the fit review. 50% starts the work, 30% is due when the first system is live by Day 21, and the final 20% remains held until the agreed proof event appears.

What will you need from our team?

One executive decision-maker, one day-to-day owner, access to the relevant experts and evidence, and prompt approvals. Plan for about three hours of founder or senior-expert time across the build.

Do you replace sales, marketing or our existing agency?

No. Education-Led Growth gives those teams one source of buyer questions, explanations and evidence to work from. Existing partners can continue specialist work when responsibilities and decision rights are clear.

What happens after day 45?

You receive a Scale / Fix / Kill recommendation and full documentation. Operate the system internally, engage Kryssen for monthly ELG operation, or stop. There is no automatic retainership.
