Before you apply.

What do we get in 45 days?

A Revenue Signal Scan, a One-Motion Plan, a live demand system by day 21, one controlled improvement, and a Scale / Fix / Kill memo your team keeps — plus the qualification or product-signal rules and measurement to run it.

What does it cost, and how is it paid?

50% to start, 30% when the motion is live by day 21, 20% held until the named proof event. The fee is set after the fit review, once the motion, access and proof target are clear.

Who does the work?

Ogunlade Oluwole and Idorenyin Idiong. Senior operators. No bait-and-switch junior team.

How many companies do you take?

Two per 45-day cycle. We accept only the number the two operators can lead directly. If the cycle is full, we say so and give the next date.

What do you need from us?

One executive decision-maker, one day-to-day revenue owner, access to relevant CRM or product data, and prompt approvals during the 45 days.

Are the logos your clients?

No. They are companies where the operators have shipped — employment, founder and advisory roles. We label that on purpose. A Kryssen-paid sprint case will be published when it exists.

What counts as proof?

A motion-specific commercial event you agree up front: accepted opportunity for sales-led, activated or paid account for PLG, or a product-qualified account progressing to revenue for hybrid.

Do you replace our sales or marketing team?

No. We make demand, product signals and qualification more useful to the team that closes and serves the customer. You keep the system.

Do you guarantee revenue?

No. We guarantee the agreed build, launch and proof process. Your product, pricing and sales execution also affect revenue. That is why 20% stays at risk — not why we write a cheque.

What happens after day 45?

You get a Scale / Fix / Kill decision and the system documentation. Keep it in-house or scope the next phase separately. No automatic retainership.
