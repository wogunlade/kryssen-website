Before you apply.

Do you guarantee revenue?

No. We guarantee the agreed build, launch and proof process. Your product, pricing and sales execution also affect revenue.

What counts as proof?

A motion-specific commercial event: accepted opportunity for sales-led, activated or paid account for PLG, or product-qualified account progressing to revenue for hybrid.

What is included?

Senior-led diagnosis, a one-motion plan, the live demand path, qualification or product-signal rules, measurement, one controlled improvement cycle and a documented Scale / Fix / Kill decision.

What must our team provide?

One executive decision-maker, one day-to-day revenue owner, access to relevant CRM or product data and prompt approvals during the 45 days.

When can an accepted engagement start?

Starts are agreed on a rolling basis after the fit review. We accept only the number of engagements the two senior operators can lead directly.

What is the investment?

We discuss investment after the fit review, once the motion, evidence, access requirements and proof target are clear. No price is published before that decision.

How selective is the programme?

We decline more applications than we accept. The Revenue Breakout is for companies with paying B2B customers or recurring product usage, an identifiable revenue break and a team ready to execute.

Do you replace sales?

No. We make demand, product signals and qualification more useful to the team that closes and serves the customer.

What happens after Day 45?

You get a Scale / Fix / Kill decision and the system documentation. Keep it in-house or scope the next phase separately.
