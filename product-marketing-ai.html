Questions before the work begins

Direct answers about this service.

What does product marketing include?

Product marketing connects the audience, problem, category, positioning, value, evidence and use cases to the material buyers and revenue teams need. It can cover launches, website messaging, sales enablement, comparisons, adoption and customer education.

When does a founder-led company need product marketing?

Product marketing becomes useful when the founder remains the best product explainer, buyers misunderstand the offer, sales and product use different language, launches lack a clear decision story or adoption depends on repeated manual explanation.

How is product marketing different from content marketing?

Product marketing defines how the market should understand, evaluate and use the product. Content marketing turns useful expertise into assets and distribution. The two work together, but they solve different parts of the buyer and customer journey.

Is product marketing only for a launch?

No. Launches are one use case. Product marketing can also support everyday positioning, sales conversations, competitive evaluation, onboarding, adoption, expansion and message governance as the product changes.

Does Kryssen guarantee product adoption or sales?

No. Kryssen can improve positioning, messages, evidence, enablement and the operating system around them. Adoption, sales and revenue also depend on product quality, pricing, market conditions and company execution.
