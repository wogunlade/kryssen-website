Questions growing businesses ask before the work begins.

What exactly will Kryssen create for us?

The exact assets depend on the buyer or customer decision, available evidence and selected engagement. The work can include website education, buyer guides, sales education, PR and executive content, LinkedIn content and customer learning. Assets, quantities, owners and delivery dates are agreed before work begins.

Do you replace our marketing team or agency?

No. Kryssen can work with internal teams and existing partners. The scope identifies which knowledge Kryssen will capture, which assets Kryssen will create, who supplies evidence and who owns review, approval and publication.

How do different teams contribute?

The agreed owner coordinates access to the people who hold useful knowledge. This may include leadership, sales, product, marketing, support, customer success, operations or another relevant team.

What happens when teams explain the business differently?

Kryssen identifies the conflicting explanations, checks them against approved expertise and evidence, and structures the agreed answer for the intended audience and use. The company retains final approval.

Which engagement should we choose?

Choose a Content Sprint for one focused decision, a Monthly Retainer for ongoing content operations or Project-Based work for a specific known requirement. You can also select “I’m not sure yet” when booking a strategy conversation.

Who owns review and approval?

The scope names one internal owner and the people responsible for evidence, expert access, review and final approval. Delivery dates depend on those responsibilities moving on schedule.

What happens after the Content Sprint?

Your team can use the finished assets internally, move to a Monthly Retainer, commission another project or stop. There is no automatic renewal.
