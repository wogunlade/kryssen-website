Before the work begins

Questions growing businesses usually ask.

Which Growth Playbook service should we start with?

Start with Content that sells when company knowledge needs to become useful buyer and customer material; AEO and GEO when stronger owned answers are needed for Google and AI discovery; Product marketing when the product story and decision journey need clarity; or Growth strategy when teams and channels need one connected plan. The services can also work together.

How do different teams contribute?

A named internal owner coordinates access to relevant people across leadership, product, sales, marketing, support, customer success or operations. Kryssen confirms evidence, reviews and approvals before work begins.

What happens when teams explain the business differently?

Kryssen identifies the conflicting explanations, checks them against approved expertise and evidence, and structures the agreed answer for the intended audience and use. The company retains final approval.

Can Kryssen work with our internal team or agency?

Yes. The scope identifies what Kryssen owns, what existing partners own, who supplies evidence and who reviews, approves and publishes the work.

How can we engage Kryssen?

Choose a Sprint for a focused priority, a Retainership for ongoing work or a Project for a defined requirement. Kryssen can also help determine the most suitable route.
