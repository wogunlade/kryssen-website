Four questions before we decide what to create.

What exactly will Kryssen create for us?

The work can include blog posts, FAQs, product or service documentation, SEO/AEO/GEO pages, website copy, customer curricula, PR material and an original industry report. The fit review identifies the smallest useful combination for the opportunity—not a compulsory bundle.

Do you replace our marketing team or agency?

No. Kryssen develops the buyer-education structure and the agreed assets. Internal teams and existing partners can continue their specialist work with clearer source material, priorities and responsibilities.

How do you decide which assets come first?

We look at the buyer decision that needs support, the questions preventing progress, the evidence available inside the company and the commercial event the business wants to observe.

What happens after the first 45 days?

Operate the documented system internally, continue with Kryssen or pause. There is no automatic retainership.
