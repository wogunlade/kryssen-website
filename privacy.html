Privacy & cookies · Effective 22 September 2026

We explain what the site collects, when measurement starts and what remains under your control.

1. Who controls your information

Kryssen LTD (“Kryssen”, “we”, “us”) controls personal data collected through kryssengrowth.com. Privacy requests can be sent to info@kryssengrowth.com.

2. Information you choose to provide

The fit-review application asks for contact and company details, audience and industry context, traction, buyer-education needs, available evidence, commercial priorities, internal ownership, readiness and source or campaign parameters. The Masterclass priority list asks for your name, work email, company, website, role, industry, content opportunity and buyer question. Email, WhatsApp and LinkedIn operate under the information you choose to send through those services.

Current replacement status: the application and Masterclass forms are connected to a protected Google Apps Script staging receiver for pre-production testing. Staging submissions are verified with Cloudflare Turnstile, stored in restricted Google Sheets and may generate test notification and receipt emails. Do not enter another person’s information during staging. This notice will be finalised with the production deployment and retention period before the replacement is merged.

3. Analytics and events

The site uses the same Google Tag Manager container identified on the current live website: GTM-NGKZZ88T. Google consent mode starts with analytics and advertising storage denied. The container can load in that restricted state, but this site suppresses non-conversion custom events until you accept optional measurement.

After acceptance, the site may record page views, section and CTA exposure, CTA clicks, WhatsApp and quiz interactions, opportunity selections and application-step activity, together with device/browser information, approximate location, referrer and campaign parameters made available by the measurement provider. We do not intentionally send application answers, workshop answers, email addresses or names as analytics event parameters.

Cloudflare may also process limited request, security and aggregate performance data when serving the website or when Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled for the Pages project. Those controls are managed in Cloudflare rather than this website repository.

4. Your cookie choice

The first visit offers Accept and Decline. Your choice is stored locally in your browser as kryssen-consent-v1, including the time it was recorded. Declining keeps analytics and marketing storage denied. Accepting grants both optional categories used by the live GTM consent setup. Use the persistent “Cookies” control to review or change the choice at any time.

5. Service providers and external links

Google Tag Manager and tags configured within it support consented measurement. Cloudflare provides hosting, delivery and security. WhatsApp and LinkedIn links take you to services with their own privacy notices. Google Apps Script processes staging form delivery, Google Sheets stores structured records and Google Mail sends operational notifications and receipts. Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent automated abuse. We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Storage, security and your rights

Consent choices remain in your browser until you change them or clear site storage. Limited session storage may preserve attribution or interface context during a visit. When production form delivery is configured, appropriate access controls, encrypted transport, retention and deletion rules must be documented here before collection begins.

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of personal data held by Kryssen, object to certain processing, withdraw consent or complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Questions, changes or removal requests

Write to info@kryssengrowth.com. We may update this notice when the form backend, measurement providers, Cloudflare settings or legal requirements change.
