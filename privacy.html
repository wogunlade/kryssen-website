Privacy policy

Here's how we manage the data we collect when you visit our website.

This notice explains what Kryssen collects, why we use it, who helps us process it and the choices available to you.

Effective: 21 August 2026

1. Who controls your information

Kryssen LTD (“Kryssen”, “we”, “us”) controls the personal data collected through kryssengrowth.com. Privacy requests can be sent to info@kryssengrowth.com.

2. What we collect

When you apply, we collect your name, role, work email, company, website, revenue motion, company stage, stated revenue constraint, current context, desired outcome and source or campaign parameters. We also process limited technical data needed for security, fraud prevention and reliable delivery, such as submission time and Turnstile verification results.

If you permit optional measurement, analytics and advertising tools may collect page views, clicks, device/browser details, approximate location, referrer, campaign parameters and cookie or similar identifiers. We do not intentionally send your form answers to analytics or advertising tools.

3. Why we use it

  • Assess whether the 45-Day Revenue Breakout fits your company.
  • Respond to your request and arrange an operator review.
  • Send your application confirmation and operational follow-up.
  • Protect the form and website from spam, fraud and abuse.
  • Measure and improve the website when you allow analytics.
  • Support consented marketing automation and understand campaign performance.

Application processing is necessary to take steps at your request before a possible engagement. Security processing supports our legitimate interest in protecting the service. Optional analytics and marketing processing rely on your consent, which you may withhold or withdraw.

4. Service providers

We use service providers only for defined operational purposes. These providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate contractual, organisational and technical safeguards.

5. Cookies and tracking choices

Necessary technologies support security and application delivery. Optional analytics and marketing tools are off unless you grant the relevant choice. You can accept, reject or manage categories through the privacy choices shown on the website, and reopen those choices at any time using the “Privacy choices” control.

6. Retention and security

Application records are normally retained for up to 12 months so we can review fit, manage follow-up and understand prior conversations, unless a longer period is needed for an active engagement, legal obligation or dispute. Consent records and essential security logs are kept only as long as reasonably needed for accountability and protection. We then delete or anonymise information where practicable.

We use access controls, encrypted transport, server-side validation, spam protection and restricted account permissions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your personal data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Email info@kryssengrowth.com with enough information for us to identify and respond to your request.

8. Links, changes and contact

Other websites linked from Kryssen operate under their own privacy notices. We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when we do.

Questions or requests: info@kryssengrowth.com.
