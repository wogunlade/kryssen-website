Questions before the work begins

Direct answers about this service.

What do AEO and GEO mean?

AEO means Answer Engine Optimization: making useful answers easier for search and answer systems to extract and represent. GEO means Generative Engine Optimization: strengthening the sources, entities, evidence and relationships generative AI systems may use when forming an answer.

Does AEO or GEO replace SEO?

No. Technical crawlability, indexation, helpful content, internal links and search intent remain the foundation. AEO and GEO build on those fundamentals by making important answers, entities and evidence clearer for people, search engines and AI-assisted discovery.

What does Kryssen change during an AEO and GEO engagement?

Kryssen can map buyer questions, clarify entities and offers, improve answer structure, strengthen source pages, connect evidence, improve internal links and prepare a technical handoff. The exact work depends on the buyer decision and current site.

How is AEO and GEO performance reviewed?

Review can combine Search Console queries and landing pages, indexation, referral traffic, branded representation and a fixed set of agreed AI prompts. AI systems vary by model, source set, location and time, so prompt observations are directional rather than a fixed score.

Can Kryssen guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. Kryssen can improve the quality, clarity and technical accessibility of owned sources, but cannot guarantee a search ranking, AI citation, recommendation or referral volume.
