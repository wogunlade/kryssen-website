Questions founders ask before the work begins.

How much founder time does the work require?

Founder time depends on the agreed scope and how much expertise is already documented. Before work begins, Kryssen names the required interviews, evidence, review responsibilities and approval dates. A named internal owner should coordinate access so every decision does not return to the founder.

Will the content still sound like me?

Kryssen works from your real explanations, language and judgment. The aim is to structure and adapt the expertise—not manufacture a different personality.

Is this ghostwriting?

Kryssen can help shape and produce founder or executive content, but it must remain grounded in real approved expertise. The company retains final approval.

What if my knowledge is not documented?

Interviews, calls, presentations, voice notes, internal documents and other team members can help capture it. The scope identifies what evidence and access are required.

Which engagement should I choose?

Choose a Content Sprint for one focused decision, a Monthly Retainer for ongoing content operations or Project-Based work for a specific known requirement. You can also select “I’m not sure yet” when booking a strategy conversation.

Can my team run the content afterwards?

Yes. The agreed handover identifies the finished assets, files and operating responsibilities. Continued work with Kryssen is optional.

What happens after the Content Sprint?

Your team can use the assets internally, move to a Monthly Retainer, commission another project or stop. There is no automatic renewal.

Your expertise should remain an advantage. Access to it should not depend entirely on you.
