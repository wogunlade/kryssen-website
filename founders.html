Before the work begins

Questions founders usually ask.

How much founder time does the work require?

Founder time depends on the scope and how much expertise is already documented. Kryssen confirms interviews, evidence, review responsibilities and approval dates before work begins.

Which Growth Playbook service should we start with?

Start with Content that sells when knowledge needs to become useful buyer and customer material; AEO and GEO when stronger owned answers are needed for Google and AI discovery; Product marketing when the product story and decision journey need clarity; or Growth strategy when the business needs one connected plan. The services can also work together.

What if my knowledge is not documented?

Kryssen can work from interviews, calls, voice notes, presentations, product documents, customer questions and other team members. The company retains final approval.

Can my team operate the work afterwards?

Yes. The agreed handover identifies the finished assets, files, ownership and operating responsibilities. Continued work with Kryssen is optional.

How can we engage Kryssen?

Choose a Sprint for a focused priority, a Retainership for ongoing work or a Project for a defined requirement. You can also ask Kryssen to help determine the most suitable route.
