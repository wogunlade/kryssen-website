Questions before the work begins

Direct answers about this service.

What is a B2B growth strategy?

A B2B growth strategy connects the commercial priority, buyer decision, product truth, evidence, channels and team responsibilities. Kryssen turns those decisions into one operating playbook across content, product marketing, discovery, sales and customer learning.

How is growth strategy different from growth marketing?

Growth strategy decides which market, buyer decision and commercial constraint the company should address and how the parts work together. Growth marketing usually focuses more narrowly on channel execution, acquisition and experimentation. Kryssen connects strategy to practical execution without treating growth as a marketing-only problem.

When should a founder bring in a growth strategy consultant?

A founder may need outside growth strategy support when positioning keeps changing, expertise remains trapped with the founder, product and marketing tell different stories, channels operate separately or the team cannot agree on the next commercial priority.

What can a Growth Strategy engagement include?

The scope can include growth-constraint diagnosis, buyer-decision mapping, positioning, product marketing, content priorities, search and AI discovery, PR, sales education, ownership, approvals and measurement. The agreed scope follows the current commercial priority.

Does Kryssen guarantee pipeline, revenue or rankings?

No. Kryssen is accountable for the agreed strategy, research, deliverables, operating clarity and review process. Rankings, AI citations, media coverage, pipeline and revenue depend on factors beyond the studio’s control and are not promised.
