Before the work begins

Questions companies usually ask.

Does Kryssen use the same Growth Playbook for every industry?

The four service areas remain available, but the buyer questions, product context, evidence, source material, distribution routes and review responsibilities change with the company and industry.

What if our industry is not listed?

Choose another industry in the strategy-conversation form and describe the buyer decision, product question or growth priority. Kryssen will review the context before deciding whether a conversation would be useful.

Do we need to know which service we need?

No. Start with the business priority. Kryssen can help determine whether Content that sells, AEO and GEO, Product marketing, Growth strategy or a combination is the most useful starting point.

How can we engage Kryssen?

Choose a Sprint for a focused priority, a Retainership for ongoing work or a Project for a defined requirement.
