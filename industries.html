Questions before the work begins.

Does Kryssen use the same content plan for every industry?

No. The operating method is consistent, but the buyer questions, available evidence, content assets, distribution routes and review responsibilities change with the company and industry.

What if our industry is not listed?

Choose another industry in the strategy-conversation form and describe the decision or customer question that needs support. Kryssen will review the context before deciding whether a conversation would be useful.

Do we need to know which service we need?

No. You can choose Content Sprint, Monthly Retainer, Project-Based or I’m not sure yet. The final scope is agreed after the decision, evidence, distribution and approval requirements are understood.
